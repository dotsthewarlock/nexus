In-modal Resume candidate lab

Six approaches to keeping the camera modal open after a nine-second preview. Every trial uses the existing Dacast URLs and cropped native-Play technique. No synthetic clicks or hidden access to player controls.

An iframe can load without video playing. The first Play tap and subsequent Resume taps are observations you must judge. Prepared players may consume data before a tap; the test automatically releases unused players after 15 seconds.

1 · Choose a candidate

First launch is identical in all six modes: 480×270 iframe, 6× crop under the Camera label, then expand that same iframe. Each Resume candidate is prepared inside the existing modal after the 9s cutoff. Candidate F is the control: a conventional button that reloads the iframe but needs an additional Dacast Play tap.

2 · Tap the Camera control

Camera
Press Prepare, then tap Camera once to start native Play.

The Camera and Resume labels do not intercept taps. The Dacast player receives them directly. A separate fallback remains available when cross-origin focus does not trigger expansion.

3 · Compare repeated sessions

Record whether the very first Resume works without another Play tap, then test two further Resume cycles on the same camera. Change candidates between trials; each transition unloads the previous player.

Event log and limitations

Iframe focus is a user-interaction signal, not a playback confirmation. The nine-second timer starts at focus for native-Play candidates; candidate F starts at its Resume button click. Idle prepared players are unloaded after 15 seconds. No third-party playback telemetry or bandwidth measurement is available.
